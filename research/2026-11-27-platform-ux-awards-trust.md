# Deep Research knowledge brief — Future online platforms, awards and trust schemes
Research date: 2026-11-27. Status: recovered research summary, not the missing full-length report. This entry records findings for Michelle Rogers Executive Agent; it is advisory knowledge, not an approval, certification, legal opinion, or authorization to launch. Sources below were cited by Deep Research; their current status has not independently been revalidated in this commit.

## 1. Future platform design
- Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the practical accessibility baseline; WCAG 3 is a forward-looking draft according to the recovered report, not a certification target. Test keyboard access, focus, screen reader semantics, contrast, responsive reflow, accessible authentication, and reduced motion.
- Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile: LCP <=2.5 s, INP <=200 ms, CLS <=0.1. Measure real-user results by device class where possible.
- Separate a universal quality layer (accessibility, responsiveness, performance, security, privacy, authentication, trust, content clarity, abuse resistance, interaction quality) from a creative signature layer (typography, layout, illustration, motion, editorial voice, spatial and domain-specific interaction).
- AI/agent interfaces should show identity, permissions, proposed actions, state, consequential confirmation, failures, logs, pause/cancel, and resulting artifacts. Avoid opaque chat-only workflows. Consider MCP and A2A interoperability without assuming they establish trust or authorize actions.
- Treat near-term measurable criteria as evidence-based; treat emerging adaptive/agentic interfaces and WCAG 3 as research directions rather than established future outcomes.

## 2. Award program research
Compared Webby, Apple Design Awards, Red Dot, iF, CES Innovation Awards, Stevie Awards, CNCF Community Awards. Recurring mechanisms: controlled winner marks, category/year, judging criteria, usage rules, organizer-controlled recipient listings, certificates, publicity and community recognition.
Create a unique private issuer identity; do not imitate government seals, accreditation or standards-body insignia or existing award marks.

## 3. Credential architecture
Badge/document fields: issuer, credential class, precise assessed scope, tier if applicable, year or criteria version, unique ID, canonical verification link/QR, current status. A certificate can additionally show recipient, issue date, expiry if applicable, criteria link, assessment scope and issuer authorization.
Use issuer-controlled public verification records; support active, expired, suspended and revoked states as applicable. Optional W3C VC 2.0 or Open Badges 3.0 payloads provide machine-readable verification. Cryptographic integrity does not prove the underlying assessment was sound. Minimize public personal data; keep evidence access controlled.

## 4. Noncash/nonphysical benefits
Public recipient directory; licensed badge use; media/communications kits; organizer amplification; professional recognition; evaluator feedback; networking; mentorship; speaking/publishing invitations; recruiting signals; verification APIs; procurement evidence packages; renewal insights. Benefits should be described accurately, not as guaranteed sales, contracts or investment.

## 5. Legal classification and governance
Distinguish ordinary excellence awards, endorsements, private trust marks, US registered certification marks, accreditation and regulated conformity assessment. The scheme's exact scope remains undecided.
- FTC: seals/endorsements can imply claims that must be substantiated; disclose material relationships and clarify scope; avoid deceptive general assurances.
- USPTO: a certification mark represents standards met by authorized third-party users; control and documented standards are essential; cancellation grounds include failure to control and discriminatory refusal to certify qualified parties. Ordinary award logos are not automatically certification marks.
- Washington: assess Consumer Protection Act exposure and My Health My Data Act applicability where covered consumer health data is processed.
- EU: GDPR Arts 42/43 certification is a specific statutory scheme; AI Act conformity assessment, European Accessibility Act and DSA interface rules require separate scope/applicability analysis.
- UK: review DMCC unfair-commercial-practice rules and ICO/UKAS formal certification/accreditation structure.
- ISO/IEC 17065 is a useful certification-body governance benchmark; 17011 relates to accreditation bodies; 42001 and 42006 relate to AI management systems and associated certification. Do not claim ISO conformity/accreditation merely from adopting principles.
- Establish versioned criteria, evidence requirements, evaluator competence, impartiality/conflict declarations, independent decisions, complaints/appeals, renewal/surveillance, suspension/revocation, mark licensing, privacy, retention, incident handling and change control. Obtain qualified legal review before selecting regulated claims, jurisdictions or a certification-mark model.

## 6. Executive agent application (new synthesis, not original research)
Use the framework to evaluate platform opportunities and prepare evidence-based partnership/award applications, identify recognition benefits, and assess the feasibility of a future award entity. Maintain a distinction between verified facts, forecasts, recommendations and legal uncertainties. Do not claim awards, accreditation or certifications not actually granted. Never publish confidential applicant or research evidence in this public repository. Start with an ordinary private recognition model only as a proposal for owner review, not a legal conclusion. Track source date, criterion version, evidence, evaluation outcome and unresolved issues. Any implementation must respect the owner's no-cost/revenue policy; no paid integration or build is authorized by this brief.
